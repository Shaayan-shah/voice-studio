# Community Guidelines

We want Voice Studio to be a respectful and constructive project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Give useful feedback focused on the work rather than the person.
- Accept reasonable corrections and differing perspectives.
- Keep discussions relevant and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove violating content and may limit or end participation when necessary. Report concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature for security issues, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile for other conduct concerns.
